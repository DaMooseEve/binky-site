Binky

A private, self-hosted personal AI assistant.

About Binky

Binky is a personal artificial intelligence project designed to assist with everyday information, communication, scheduling and organisation.

Binky uses locally operated AI infrastructure and can connect to authorised services such as Google Gmail, Contacts and Calendar.

Privacy by design

Binky is developed for personal use. Its conversational and connected-service information is managed within privately operated systems rather than published through this website.

Development

Binky is an independently developed personal AI project. Features and integrations are being developed incrementally.
