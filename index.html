Your home. Your information.

Binky keeps your private life private.

The Binky computer is installed in your home. The copies it saves, the notes it makes and the work it does are kept there, rather than being sent away to a shared artificial-intelligence service.

Binky uses strong, practical protections: only approved people and services are allowed in, information is locked while it travels, and access can be withdrawn. No computer can honestly promise perfect security, but Binky is designed to keep your information as safe as reasonably possible.
