About the project

Binky is an independently developed, self-hosted personal AI assistant intended for private, single-user operation.

This website provides information about the project. It does not offer public access to the Binky assistant.

Authorised integrations

Binky may connect to Google Gmail, Contacts and Calendar, and other services, using permissions granted by the account holder.

Connected services are accessed solely for the personal information management and assistance functions configured by the operator.

Automated actions

When enabled, Binky may automatically identify appointments and create, update or cancel calendar events. Automated interpretations can occasionally be incorrect.

The operator remains responsible for reviewing important appointments and decisions.

Availability and accuracy

Binky is an experimental personal software project provided without guarantees of availability, accuracy or uninterrupted operation.

AI-generated interpretations and recommendations should be checked before relying on them for important decisions.
