Binky Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

About Binky

Binky is a privately operated, single-user AI assistant designed for personal information management, communication and scheduling.

Information accessed

With authorisation, Binky can access Gmail messages and metadata, Google Contacts, and Google Calendar events. Calendar integration is under development and may include creating, updating and cancelling events when enabled.

Future integrations may include SMS, WhatsApp and Messenger. These services are not currently connected through this website.

How information is used

Information is used to retrieve and organise communications, identify relevant contacts, answer questions, recognise appointments and maintain personal calendar information.

Binky uses self-hosted AI models for processing. Google user data is not used to train general-purpose AI models, sold, or used for advertising.

Storage and security

Connected-service information, conversation history and processing records are stored on privately operated computer systems, primarily in local SQLite databases.

AI processing may take place across privately operated computers connected through a private network. Authentication credentials are stored separately from conversation records.

Access to the underlying systems is restricted. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Data sharing

Binky does not sell personal information or disclose Google user data to advertisers. Information is exchanged with authorised service providers only as necessary for the requested integrations, or where required by law.

Retention and deletion

Information may be retained in local storage until removed by the system operator. Removing Google access does not automatically erase previously synchronised local records.

Google account access can be revoked through Google Account settings. The system operator can remove locally stored information and associated credentials.

Google API data

Binky adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements.

Public website

This informational website does not provide public access to private messages, calendars or Binky databases. It does not intentionally include advertising or analytics trackers. The website hosting provider may maintain standard access logs.

Privacy enquiries

Contact the project maintainer through the Binky project repository. Do not include sensitive personal information in public GitHub issues.
